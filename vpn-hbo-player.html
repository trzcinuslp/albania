VPN for HBO / Player on your phone

Free apps rarely work for geo-blocked streaming. Paid VPNs with Poland (and US) servers are what current reviews say actually unlock Player.pl and Max/HBO.

Verdict: For Player.pl and Max/HBO, use a paid VPN with Poland servers. Free ones are fine for a quick test — most get blocked or run out of data.

How to connect

Server country must match the service you’re unlocking.

  1. 1
    Player.pl → connect to a VPN server in Poland (Warsaw if listed).
  2. 2
    Max / HBO → server in the country of your subscription (e.g. US or Poland).
  3. 3
    Connect VPN → force-close Max/Player → reopen the app.
  4. 4
    If blocked → switch to another server in the same country before changing apps.

Bottom line

Free: start with Proton or Windscribe — quick test, low expectations.

Paid that should work: NordVPN first, then Surfshark or ExpressVPN.

Success changes week to week as services block IPs. If one server fails, try another in the same country.
